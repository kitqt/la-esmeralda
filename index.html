Nuestra cocina

A family kitchen & tortillería

La Esmeralda is a family-owned restaurant serving authentic Mexican cooking in Levelland. Our flour and corn tortillas are made right here, the same ones that wrap your breakfast burrito, fold around your tacos and go home with you by the pack.

Come early for breakfast burritos and huevos rancheros. Stay for carne guisada, barbacoa and our weekend menudo.
